# Conversations and memory

## History, notes and People have different roles

Conversation history retains messages. With access, Kejtu can search prior
conversations and read exact source text. A model request has a finite context;
all saved messages are not automatically included in every reply. Search results
and automatic recall may contain only a selection. Kejtu should read relevant
originals before making specific claims about what was said.

The notebook holds separately authored, versioned notes. Kejtu may save useful
continuity when note writing is allowed; a note is not necessarily a verbatim
message or an independently verified fact. Open **Settings → This conversation
→ Notes** to inspect current notes, revisions and available origins. The message
menu's **Save a memory…** lets you write a note linked to that question.
The historical notebook collection appears only when older imported notes are
available. A fresh installation has no old notebook entry.

People stores structured information about people and relationships with source
links. **Permissions & recall** controls its conversational access. Being able to
remember a name does not grant access to email, contacts or somebody else's files.

## Your controls

**Settings → Privacy & access → Permissions & recall** contains People, reminder,
task and preference-recall choices. Notes and conversation controls have their
own panels. A permission setting and a record's existence are separate: turning
access off does not delete the record. Existing conversations and per-source
access still matter. Kejtu should not infer that it has unrestricted memory.

## Exclude or delete an exchange

Open the **Message actions** menu beside a question:

- **Exclude from recall** keeps the visible transcript and excludes the exchange
  from future recall. Recorded dependent material may also be excluded.
- **Delete exchange** removes that question, its reply/partial reply and owned
  execution records from the active archive. It does not delete the whole thread.
  Later replies, independent notes and other records keep their own text,
  including quotations. References to removed originals become unavailable.

Deletion is a local user action; Kejtu has no chat tool for deleting messages.
The confirmation explains its scope. A failed or unconfirmed operation must not
be described as a successful deletion. Pending operations can be inspected and
reconciled without automatically repeating them.

Deleting a notebook note is a separate operation with its own preview and checks;
it may refuse when retained copies require a copy-handling decision. Exchange
deletion is not a promise to erase all mentions, backups, exported files, external
services or disk-level remnants. Review those separately if that is your goal.
